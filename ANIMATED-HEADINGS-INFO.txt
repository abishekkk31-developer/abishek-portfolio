Animated section headings have been added to About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications, and Contact.

Animation behavior:
- Tag enters first with a soft scale + blur reveal.
- Title rises into place with a smooth blur/fade.
- Subtitle follows with a shorter delayed reveal.
- A small underline sweeps in.
- Animations run once per section and remain fully visible afterward.
- No section heading relies on permanent opacity:0.
